feat(ads): drop Adsterra, add ExoClick popunder + instant message zones

Two new ExoClick zones, and the Adsterra integration removed in the same
change so the site runs on a single ad stack.

WHY ADSTERRA CAME OUT

Two script tags were integrated in 05e092d. Their CDN
(profitableratecpmnetwork.com) could not be reached from this machine by
any method tried: direct request, real IP pinned with SNI preserved,
and two neutral third-party fetchers. Local DNS returns 1.1.1.1 for those
hosts while public DNS returns the real IPs, which is the signature of
DNS-level interference on this network. We could confirm the tags were
injected but never confirm delivery or earnings, and an unverifiable ad
tag is not worth carrying.

ExoClick is verifiable: s.magsrv.com responds from the same shell, and
every zone below was watched filling in the browser console before this
commit was written.

ZONE 6057172 - POPUNDER (new, site-wide)

This is the unit that monetises a visit with no engagement. Everything
else in the stack is conditional on the visitor doing something: pre-roll
needs a play click, out-stream needs a pause, slider needs browse time,
sponsored grid needs scrolling past it. A visitor who lands and leaves
earns nothing, and that is most of the top of the funnel.

Mounted in the root layout on every route, /watch included. That is a
deliberate reversal of the rule every other unit follows -
ExoclickVideoSlider excludes /watch because a floating video player in a
full-bleed playback screen is hostile to what we sell. The popunder is
not the same: trigger_method 3 fires on user interaction rather than on
load, and frequency_period 60 / frequency_count 1 caps it at one per
visitor per hour via their own cookie. It cannot ambush someone at the
moment they press play. We deliberately add no second cap, because two
caps on one format means neither is the one actually governing.

The dashboard issued this as a raw inline <script>, not an <ins> tag:
the popunder is a self-contained popMagic v12 IIFE with its own adConfig
pointing at a.pemsrv.com / s.pemsrv.com, a completely different delivery
host from the a.magsrv.com loader the other zones use. The ~16KB of
minified vendor code is shipped verbatim as public/popunder-6057172.js
and loaded by src, rather than inlined into a JSX string body where
React's children handling would have to survive that much obfuscated
third-party code. Do not reformat it; regenerate from the dashboard
snippet if the zone is ever reissued.

ZONE 6057176 - INSTANT MESSAGE (new, detail pages)

Same ad-provider delivery as the slider and sponsored grid, so it is an
<ins> plus one AdProvider push. It renders a single native message
rather than a four-card row, so it is placed on the movie and TV detail
pages - the deepest-intent surfaces we have, and the only pages that
carried no native inventory at all - rather than crowding the six browse
pages that already spend vertical space on SponsoredRow.

No suppression cap, deliberately. See SponsoredRow: the suppression key
used to be written before the ad was ever requested, so a no-fill or a
blocked loader burned the slot for the session while nothing had been
shown. ExoClick caps per zone on their side; a second cap here would only
suppress fill we could have earned.
